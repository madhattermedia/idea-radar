Idea Radar. What to say about what happened this week.

A free system for Claude. It works out what you believe, watches the news in your field, and fills a spreadsheet with content ideas. Each one comes with an angle that is yours and a hook to open with.

Install it Download the zip

The news is the same for everyone. Your take on it is the content.

Most people who post regularly run out of things to say, or end up repeating the headline everyone else has already shared. The fix is not more ideas. It is a reason to post today, plus something only you would say about it.

This is a cut-down version of the idea radar we run twice a week for our clients. Theirs feeds a planning dashboard. Yours feeds a spreadsheet, which is all you need to start.

How it works

Once · about ten minutes
The interview

Claude asks about your field, who you want to reach and what you believe. If you are not sure what you believe yet, it helps you find out. Then it suggests three to six pillars to watch and saves your profile.

"Set up my idea radar"
Whenever you want ideas
The scan

Claude researches each pillar, opens and checks every story, and adds the best ones to your spreadsheet with an angle and a hook. A full scan takes a few minutes.

Install

You need Claude Code: either in the terminal, or the Code tab of the Claude desktop app. Web search needs to be available. It is not built for the ordinary Claude chat window, because your profile and spreadsheet live in a folder on your computer.

Recommended · you get updates

As a plugin

  1. In Claude Code, run this:
    /plugin marketplace add madhattermedia/idea-radar
  2. Then this:
    /plugin install idea-radar@mad-hatter
  3. Start a new session and say "set up my idea radar".
No plugin system needed

From the zip

  1. Download idea-radar.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open a terminal in the unzipped idea-radar folder and run:
    mkdir -p ~/.claude/skills && cp -R skills/radar-setup skills/radar-scan ~/.claude/skills/
  3. Start a new Claude session and say "set up my idea radar".

Not comfortable in a terminal? Open the unzipped folder in the Claude desktop app's Code tab and ask Claude to install the two skills for you.

Using it

"Set up my idea radar"Runs the interview and saves your profile.
"Run my radar"Scans your pillars and adds new ideas to the sheet.
"Change my pillars"Updates your profile. "Add a belief" works too.
"Write a script for row 3"Not part of the radar, but Claude can read the sheet and draft from any row.

Where your files are

Everything sits in a folder called idea-radar in your home folder. profile.md is your beliefs and pillars, and you can edit it by hand. ideas.csv is the spreadsheet. It opens in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets.

Getting good results

  • Give it something to read first. Paste your website or a few old posts at the start of the interview. Correcting a draft is easier than answering from a blank page.
  • Disagree with it. When it writes your beliefs back, sharpen the soft ones. A belief is useful when someone in your industry could argue with it.
  • Give it proof. Two or three true facts about your business make the angles specific. It will never make one up, so without them it leaves [gaps in brackets] for you.
  • Use the Status and Notes columns. They are yours. Scans only ever add rows.
  • Run it on a rhythm. Twice a week suits most fields. If your Claude app supports scheduled tasks, ask it to "run my radar every Monday and Thursday at 7am".

Questions

Does it cost anything?

The radar is free. It runs inside your own Claude plan, so a scan uses some of your normal usage, roughly what a few minutes of research would.

Will it make things up?

It is built not to. Every story has to be opened and checked before it goes on the sheet, and a story it cannot open is left out. It does not invent statistics or facts about your business. You should still click the link before you post about anything.

Where does my information go?

Your profile and spreadsheet stay in a folder on your computer. Nothing is sent to us. The research itself runs through your Claude account like any other conversation.

A scan came back with only a few ideas. Is it broken?

Probably not. A quiet week in a pillar gets fewer ideas, because it will not pad the sheet. Some news sites also block automated reading. If the same belief keeps finding nothing, ask Claude to change your pillars.

Does it write the scripts?

No. It gives you the idea, the angle and the opening line. You can film from that, or hand a row back to Claude and ask for a draft.

Can I change it?

Yes. The whole thing is a few plain text files, and the source is on GitHub.

Would you prefer someone took it from idea to finished video?

That is what we do. We plan, film and edit content for founders and their teams, and an idea radar like this one is where our clients' videos start.
